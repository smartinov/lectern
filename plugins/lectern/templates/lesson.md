# Lesson title {#lesson-slug}

Open with a concrete problem, prediction, or decision that the lesson resolves.

::: objectives
After this lesson, you can:

- State an observable outcome and its course objective ID.

## What is happening

Explain the mechanism using one coherent example. Connect concrete observations
to the underlying principle. Define prerequisites when first needed.

## Work through it

Show a worked example, including why each consequential step follows. Then
provide a partly completed example and an independent application. Explain
where any analogy stops matching the subject.

## Recap

Connect the lesson's explanation back to the opening problem. Include an
earlier taught objective when this is a later lesson.

Support substantive claims with links such as [research notes](#source-source-id).
The explanation itself must be sufficient without opening those links.
